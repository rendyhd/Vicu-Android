# Vicu 3.0.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 3.0.0 brings your calendar to the Plan screen, and Vicu Pro, an optional set of tools for
people who lead work with others. Pro is free, built in and off until you turn it on. With Pro off,
Vicu works exactly as before. Update the desktop app to 3.0.0 too, so both apps read events, Pro's
data and the new list conditions the same way.

### Events and calendars

- **Your events on the Plan screen.** Add your own events, one-off or repeating, and see them next
  to your time blocks. Move them and change their length like blocks; a repeating event asks which
  times the change is for.
- **Your phone's calendars** show on Plan, read only and on this device. Busy only shows busy time
  without titles.
- **Plan my day** counts events as busy time.
- **Import a calendar file** into your own events, with Undo.

### Vicu Pro

Turn it on in Settings, General, Pro. [What Pro does on the phone](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/blob/main/docs/pro.md).

- **Know what you can do now.** Lists can show only what is available now, and each row says why a
  task is not: it starts later, waits on another task, is later in a sequential project, or is on
  hold. Start dates (`starts monday`), After, flags (`!flag`), estimates (`~30m`), project types and
  statuses, and a Flagged view.
- **Promises and people.** Mark a label as a person, then `Jan: send the deck friday` is Jan's
  promise to you and `for Jan: ...` yours to Jan. Promises due come first on Today: tap the person's
  mark for the verdict. Waiting on, People and a page per person with the last 90 days in plain
  words, a 1:1 agenda to share, and a recap.
- **The desktop's Pro tools** (the portfolio, meetings, import, lists and the weekly review) are on
  the desktop app; the phone keeps their data and follows it.

Pro keeps its records in one hidden task in your Inbox on your own server, like Vicu's other synced
data. Turning Pro off writes nothing.
