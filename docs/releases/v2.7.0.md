# Vicu 2.7.0 for Android and iOS

Vicu 2.7.0 adds a day planner, task templates and filters in Search. Comments can hold files, images open inside the app, and long notes and long lists are easier to move through. Update the desktop app to 2.7.0 too, so both apps agree on time blocks, templates and subtask order.

### New

- **Plan your day.** Give tasks a time on a timeline and plan a day from what you have. It is off until you turn it on in Settings, General, Plan, and it is set per device. Plan shows one day at a time with a strip of seven days and a tab at the right edge with the tasks to plan. Tap a task and then a time, or press and hold it and drag it. Press and hold a block to move it and drag its handle to change the length. Routines show as small pills at their times, and Plan can take a bottom bar slot. Today gets a List and Day switch. [How it works](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/blob/v2.7.0/docs/planner.md).
- **Plan my day.** A short session, from the sun button on Plan, that takes you through leftovers, choosing what to do and arranging it. Nothing is placed until you accept, and Undo all puts it back. It can remind you when each block starts. With the assistant set up it suggests times and lengths, and it works fully without. An evening notification can offer it for tomorrow.
- **Time blocks from quick add.** "Call Sam tomorrow at 3pm for 30m" makes a task with that time set aside, whether the planner is on or not. A block shows on its row and puts the task on Today on that day, also in the widgets, the daily summary and Ask. It moves with the due date, a duplicate keeps it, and a new task's default reminder goes by the block's start.
- **Task templates.** Save as template in a task's menu keeps its project, notes, labels, priority, repeat, due date as days from today, reminders that count from the due date, and subtasks. Quick add then has a last chip that lists your templates; a tap makes the task and opens it with its title selected, and a long press offers Edit and Remove. Templates reach your other devices. ([#84](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/84))
- **Filters in Search.** Chips under the search field narrow by date, status, labels and priority, for that search, with or without text. ([#96](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/96))
- **Files in comments.** Attach a file to a comment, or paste a screenshot with the Paste image chip or Ctrl or Cmd+V. On Android a long press's Paste and the keyboard's clipboard work too. Images show in the thread and open in the viewer, and other files offer Open, Save and Share. A file deleted from the task reads "No longer available".
- **Images open in the app.** An image attached to a task has a thumbnail and opens in a viewer with Save and Share. Other image types, such as HEIC, still open in another app.
- **Move subtasks.** Press and hold a subtask to move it, with Move up and Move down for TalkBack. The order is kept for your account, so other people and Vikunja's web app keep the server's order.
- **Change a date in Ask.** A due date on a proposal card can be changed before you apply it, and the assistant is told what you changed. ([#88](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/88))
- **Notes in dictation.** Dictation's draft cards now have notes you can edit. ([#95](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/95))
- **Scrollbars.** Long lists have a scrollbar while they scroll, and its thumb can be dragged. ([#78](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/78))

### Fixes

- **Long notes.** The task editor is now one scrolling page, so you can always tell where you are. Long notes fold at half the visible height with **Show all**, and a tap in the text opens them. ([#90](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/90))
- **Press and hold in notes.** It no longer drags lines around and now selects text on the line you are editing. Move line up and down in the toolbar move list and checklist lines, and **Copy notes** in the menu copies the whole note. ([#91](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/91))
- **Labels in the editor.** All of a task's labels show instead of two and "+N". They stay in the row when they fit on one line, and otherwise move below it. ([#77](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/77))
- **Task title.** A one-line title now lines up with its circle. ([#89](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/89))
- **Adding subtasks.** The field stays above the keyboard while you add several in a row. ([#92](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/92))
- **Quick add.** The title has the full width. The expand, send and clear buttons moved to the row with Notes, directly above the keyboard.
- **New tasks stay at the end.** A new task could sit at the top of its list until the next refresh, and a task created just as the connection timed out was never moved.
- **Dragging in a project.** A drag between tasks could be gone after reopening the project, and could move the task to the top of its list. A drag now plans from the list's real positions, and if that fails the row goes back and the message says why.
- **At night.** The snackbar, the "Hold + to say a task" hint and Plan's banner are no longer nearly white in a dark theme.
- **Snackbar buttons.** Open and Undo sit beside the text unless the font is set large.
- **When buttons.** Today, Tomorrow, This weekend and Next week now sit under the notes in the editor, not between the title and the notes.
- **Widget count.** The task widget's total is the real count and no longer stops at 20.
- **Project picker.** The children of a project whose parent is archived now stay nested below it.
- **Themed icon.** The Android themed launcher icon is solid and no longer shows as a faded square, with thanks to [@RVZO6](https://github.com/RVZO6). ([#83](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/pull/83))
