# Vicu Android 1.10.0

Vicu Android 1.10.0 ships together with Vicu 1.10.0 for the desktop. Both apps now share one design system, so colours, text, spacing, dates and motion match. Completing tasks is calmer, picking dates is faster, and bulk actions are more reliable.

### A new look, shared with the desktop app

- Vicu colours are now the default. If you never changed the setting, the app switches from your wallpaper colours to the Vicu colours. To get your wallpaper colours back, open **Settings**, go to **Appearance**, and turn on **Use device colours** (Android 12 and later). With it on, status and swipe colours are adjusted to blend with your wallpaper palette.
- Text that is easy to read in light and dark themes. Label chips are tinted pills whose text always has enough contrast.
- Each smart list has its own icon and colour in the drawer, the bottom bar and the widget picker.
- Task rows have a title line and a quieter line below with the project, labels and date, and a bell and priority at the end. Sections have two levels of headers with counts, and a group of one gets no header.
- Priority is shown with bars (one to three for low, medium and high) and a filled square for urgent, with the name read out by TalkBack. This replaces the exclamation marks, and the same marks are used in the widget.
- Dates read the same way in rows, chips, headers, the editor and the widget, and follow your phone's 12 or 24 hour clock in every sheet.
- Upcoming is grouped by day with sticky day headers, and by project within each day.
- The Logbook is grouped by the day you completed tasks. Review rows have a **Mark reviewed** button.
- Large titles fold into the top bar as you scroll, and the **New task** button shrinks to an icon.
- Vicu asks for notification permission after setup and explains why.

### Completing tasks

- A completed task stays in the list for 5 seconds, longer while you touch it, so you can still change your mind. Then the row collapses.
- One **Completed** message with **Undo** appears. Complete several tasks in a row and it reads "3 completed". It pauses while you touch it.
- The checkbox animates and you feel a small tap when it completes.
- Undo of a repeating task puts its dates back.

### Picking a date: the When sheet

One sheet for every date and time, also when you swipe a task to schedule it:

- Type a date in your own words ("tomorrow 9am", "next friday").
- Quick choices, with the coming weekdays listed by name.
- A calendar docked in the sheet.
- Times, shown in your phone's clock style.

### Quick add

- A compact sheet that sits right above the keyboard, with one row of chips.
- The chips follow what you type (project, labels, date, priority) unless you set them yourself, and typed tokens move into chips.

### The task editor

The editor now reads like a task: an editable headline, plain notes, and one row of property chips for the date, project, priority, repeat and reminders. It fades and rises in, follows the back gesture as you swipe, and saves the moment you dismiss it.

### Swiping and moving

- Swipe actions use the Vicu colours, show a word beside the icon, deepen their tint the further you swipe, and give a tap at the point where the action commits.
- Reordering ticks as the task passes each position.

### Motion

- Tabs fade through, and opening a project slides in from the side (mirrored for right-to-left languages).
- Predictive back shows where you are going before you let go.
- Lists move smoothly when rows are added, completed or reordered.
- Counts roll when they change. When you empty Today, it says **All clear** and offers the next task.
- With animations turned off in Android, all of this becomes a plain fade or nothing at all.

### Routines

- Routines are now optional. **Settings**, **General**, **Routines** has **Enable routines** and **Show routines in Today**, both on by default. With routines off they leave the drawer and Today and their reminders stay quiet; your routines and their history are kept.
- Today lists only the routines still open for the day. Completed and skipped ones leave the section, and the section disappears when nothing is left, the same as on the desktop.

### Projects

The drawer shows a progress ring for each project, from its completed and open tasks.

### More reliable

- Changes are sent one at a time. Bulk actions on many tasks no longer lose changes on Vikunja servers that use SQLite ("database is locked").
- A task that was deleted on the server, or on another device, disappears from your lists without needing a full refresh.

### Known issues

- The calendar in the When sheet starts the week on Sunday; the rest of Vicu, and the desktop app, start on Monday.
- Undo of a repeating task that was completed while offline does not put its dates back.

### Upgrade notes

- Update both apps to 1.10.0 to get the same look and the same date wording on both.
- Vicu Android needs a Vikunja server running version 2.4.0 or newer.
